Rules & How to Play

Overall

Each round uses a freshly shuffled 100-card deck. Every player starts with 5 face-down shields and 9 cards in hand.

There are 4 rounds. Player 1 starts Round 1, Player 2 starts Round 2, Player 3 starts Round 3, and Player 4 starts Round 4. Within each round, the lead rotates clockwise every turn. Eliminated players are skipped.

Order of Play

1. The leader draws 1 card.

2. Starting with the leader and moving clockwise, each player may play 1 effect.

3. That player then plays 1 unit.

4. A player may skip the effect and play a unit immediately.

5. If a player has no unit after playing an effect, they may play a second effect instead, but they automatically lose that turn.

6. After everyone has played, compare total power. The highest power wins. Ties for highest power also count as winners.

7. Every losing player loses 1 shield.

Effects

+1 to +4: Add that amount to your unit's power for the current turn.

Draw 1 / Draw 2: Draw that many cards immediately.

Shield Saver: Played as your effect and stays on your field as an extra shield.

Negate: Has no target. It nullifies the next effect played later that turn, then is used up. If no later effect is played, it expires at the end of the turn.

Destroy: Destroys itself and one valid previously played effect. It can also be nullified by Negate.

Extra Rules

Original face-down shields are always lost first and go into that player's hand. Shield Savers are lost only after all original shields are gone, and broken Shield Savers go to the discard pile.

Each shield broken is worth 1 point to every winner of that turn.

Once a player has no shields left, their next lost turn eliminates them from the round.

The last player standing ends the round. Scores carry across all 4 rounds, and the highest total score wins the match.
